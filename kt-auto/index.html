Smog check

Got a DMV notice that says "STAR station"? You're in the right place.

K·T is a State of California licensed, STAR Certified smog check station, so we can test the cars DMV sends to STAR stations as well as everyday renewals. If your car fails, we can find the problem and fix it here instead of sending you somewhere else.

  • Bring your DMV renewal notice and the car
  • Check-engine light on? Call first and we'll take a look
  • Classic and older cars welcome
